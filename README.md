What is NASA RASC-AL:
NASA RASC-AL (Revolutionary Aerospace Systems Concepts – Academic Linkage) is an annual, university-level engineering design competition sponsored by NASA and the National Institute of Aerospace that challenges student teams to design bold new concepts for space exploration. The "Sustained Lunar Evolution – An Inspirational Moment" theme in the 2025 NASA RASC-AL challenged university teams to design scalable, long-term infrastructure and operational concepts for humanity's permanent return to the Moon.
